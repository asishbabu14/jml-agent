# Reset MFA

IT can reset an Okta factor only after a person confirms who you are.

1. Tell the help desk which email address cannot sign in.
2. A teammate in IT approves the reset after a video call, an in-person check, or a call with your manager.
3. Sign in again and enroll a new factor when Okta asks.
4. The assistant cannot reset a factor by itself.
