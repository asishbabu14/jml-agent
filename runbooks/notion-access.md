# I can't get into Notion

1. Confirm you are in the app-notion group. The assistant can look that up.
2. If you are not in the group, ask for access. That request waits for IT.
3. If you are in the group and still cannot sign in, reset is the wrong first step. Open a ticket and include the email address you typed.
4. This lab's Notion tile is a bookmark on a fake URL. There is no real Notion workspace behind it.
